About this project

This site is an academic project — a thesis prototype for a tourist-guide web application. "Undiscovered Macedonia", its guides, trips, departure dates and statistics are invented for the demonstration. Nothing here is a real company and no booking is possible.

Photography

Images are used here for academic demonstration only and are placeholders. They come from stock-image previews and public web results and are not licensed for commercial use. Before any real deployment they would be replaced with properly licensed photography (for example from Unsplash or Pexels, both free for commercial use) or with the project's own photographs.

Subjects photographed include Matka Canyon, Lake Ohrid and the Church of St. John at Kaneo, Lake Mavrovo and the submerged church of St. Nicholas, Pelister National Park, the Šar Mountains and Popova Šapka, Bitola's Široko Sokak, Skopje's Old Bazaar and Stone Bridge, Bigorski Monastery, the Painted Mosque in Tetovo, the Galičnik Wedding, Macedonian food, and Balkan wildlife (lynx, bear, wolf, wild horses).

Written content

Descriptive facts about places, food, wildlife, national parks and traditions were compiled and paraphrased from public tourism sources, primarily:

  • macedoniatourism.com — the official tourism website
  • macedoniatravel.com — tour structure and categories
  • visitmacedonia.mk — destinations and archaeological sites

All marketing copy, guide profiles, trip itineraries and the schedule are original and fictional.

Code & libraries

  • Hand-written HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript — no framework.
  • Leaflet (BSD-2) with OpenStreetMap tiles for the map.
  • Anton and Epilogue from Google Fonts (SIL Open Font License).
  • The "UMac" assistant, when enabled, calls a language model through a local proxy (see ai-proxy/). Offline it uses a small built-in knowledge base.
  • The "Meet Travellers" feature is a front-end prototype; profiles are sample data and all state is stored only in the visitor's browser.

Data & privacy

Nothing you type on this site is sent anywhere. The "Plan a trip" form, saved itineraries and the Meet Travellers profile are all kept in your browser's localStorage and can be cleared at any time from your browser settings.
