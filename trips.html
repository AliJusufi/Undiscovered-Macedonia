The Grand Loop

12 days

Skopje · Matka · Mavrovo · Ohrid · Bitola · Tikveš · Skopje

If you come to Macedonia once, this is the trip. It takes in the capital and the canyon, the western mountains, three unhurried nights on Lake Ohrid, the café life of Bitola, and a last night in the wine country before the airport.

  • Days 1–2 — Skopje: the Old Bazaar, the fortress, and a half-day out to Matka to kayak.
  • Days 3–4 — Mavrovo: Bigorski Monastery, the Radika valley, a walk on Mount Bistra.
  • Days 5–7 — Ohrid: the lakeside churches, a boat to St. Naum, a free day.
  • Days 8–9 — Bitola & Pelister: Heraclea, and a hike to the glacial lakes.
  • Days 10–11 — Tikveš: two wineries, the ruins at Stobi, a long lunch.
  • Day 12 — back to Skopje for the flight.

Balanced pace · culture & nature · led by Marko or Ana

Lakes & Peaks Walking

8 days

Ohrid · Galičica · Pelister · Šar foothills

Day hikes at a real pace, with one hut-to-hut section in the middle. Nothing technical — good boots and a normal level of fitness are enough. We move your bags between hotels so you walk with a daypack.

  • Days 1–2 — Ohrid: settle in, a shakeout walk above the lake.
  • Days 3–4 — Galičica: the ridge between Ohrid and Prespa, wild horses on the tops.
  • Days 5–6 — Pelister: hut to hut across Baba Mountain to Pelister's Eyes.
  • Day 7 — the Šar foothills above Tetovo.
  • Day 8 — transfer to Skopje.

Active · moderate hiking · led by Jon or Ardit

The South, Slowly

6 days

Ohrid · Struga · Galičica · St. Naum

Almost no driving. Lake mornings, frescoes, the springs at St. Naum by boat, one gentle walk on Galičica, and a winery lunch. For people who'd rather do one place properly than five in a hurry.

  • Days 1–3 — Ohrid: the old town, Kaneo, Samuel's Fortress, a swim.
  • Day 4 — Struga and the Black Drim quay; afternoon free.
  • Day 5 — boat to St. Naum, short walk on Galičica, winery lunch on the way back.
  • Day 6 — transfer to Ohrid or Skopje airport.

Relaxed · easy walking · led by Petar

The Wild West

7 days

Mavrovo · Bigorski · Radika valley · Popova Šapka

The corner of the country almost nobody visits. Woodcarvings at Bigorski, the Radika river, wildlife-watching in Mavrovo National Park, and a day up on the Šar plateau. Runs in a winter version with skiing and snowshoes.

  • Days 1–2 — Mavrovo village, the submerged church, an evening wildlife watch.
  • Days 3–4 — Bigorski Monastery and walks in the Radika valley.
  • Days 5–6 — up to Popova Šapka on the Šar plateau; Tetovo and the Painted Mosque.
  • Day 7 — transfer to Skopje.

Active · mountains & wildlife · led by Ardit

Experiences

Most trips end up with two or three of these worked in. Tell us which and we'll build around them.

  • 01

    Hiking the national parks

    The Galičica ridge between two lakes, Pelister's glacial lakes and molika pine, the summits around Mavrovo. Graded to your group.

  • 02

    Kayaking & lake days

    The Ohrid shoreline, or the still green water of Matka, with a stop at a cave or a monastery you can only reach by boat.

  • 03

    Winter & snow

    Ski or snowshoe at Mavrovo, then thaw out in a village guesthouse under the peaks.

  • 04

    Food & wine

    Tikveš cellars, Ohrid trout, tavče gravče in a village kitchen, and lunches that run into the afternoon.

  • 05

    River days

    Guided kayak runs on the bright Treska below Matka, and gentle floats on the Crn Drim out of Struga.
